# Popover

Non-modal content anchored to a trigger: a small details panel, a definition, or the touch stand-in for a tooltip.

```tsx
import {
  Popover,
  PopoverArrow,
  PopoverClose,
  PopoverDescription,
  PopoverPopup,
  PopoverSource,
  PopoverTitle,
  PopoverTrigger,
} from '@fairgarden/design/overlays/popover'
```

The panel renders in a portal as a `white` scope that follows the page mode, whatever the trigger's ground. Its `--border-size-2` `--primary12` frame carries the boundary in both modes, with `--radius-2-25` and no shadow. It sits 8 px from the trigger, aligned to its start edge, and flips before it would clip. It is 240 to 360 px wide from 768 px, and the viewport less its margins below. It opens on press, never on hover, from its trigger by the outline morph, or instantly; Esc or an outside press closes it. Popovers are hidden in print.

Keep essential instructions out of a popover. A filter panel with more than two controls, or anything that needs confirmation, belongs in a Dialog.

## Panel and definition

import { DemoPopoverKinds } from './demos/kinds'

<DemoPopoverKinds.Title />

`kind="panel"` (the default) is a padded panel with a title in `type-subhead`; put `PopoverClose` first so the title wraps beside it. `kind="definition"` sets the term in tracked caps, the definition in `type-body-ui` and an optional `PopoverSource` caption. `PopoverArrow` adds the tail, whose edge continues the frame. Open one from the keyboard: the trigger's ring grows into the frame, and the tail grows out of the frame's edge as it lands, then retracts into it as the panel closes.

## Across triggers

import { DemoPopoverMorph } from './demos/morph'

<DemoPopoverMorph.Title />

The outline morph is on by default: the trigger's focus ring grows into the panel's frame as it opens and shrinks back as it closes. Opened by pointer, the trigger's edge grows instead: an `outline` Button's edge, a `solid` Button's box in its fill, or, for a `text` Button, the box its focus ring draws, in its ink. A popover opened on hover appears at once. `morph={false}` on Popover opens and closes the panel at once; see [Outline Morph](../../foundations/outline-morph/page.mdx). The last trigger sits at the foot of a tall stage, so with the demo near the top of the window its panel opens upward.

## Tails on every side

import { DemoPopoverSides } from './demos/sides'

<DemoPopoverSides.Title />

`side` puts the panel above, below, left or right of its trigger, and the tail sits on the edge facing it. The tail grows out of the frame's edge on whichever side that is.
